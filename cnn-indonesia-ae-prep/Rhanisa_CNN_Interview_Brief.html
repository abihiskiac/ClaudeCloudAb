The role

What an Account Executive does, in order

You’ll sell to two kinds of buyer: media agencies (a planner shortlists channels, a buyer negotiates price) and direct clients such as brands, BUMN, ministries and regional governments. The highlighted step is where a banker stands out.

  1. Prospect and plan accountsMap agencies and direct clients in your categories.
  2. Read the briefObjective, target audience, period, budget, KPIs.
  3. Build the proposalWith programming, digital, creative and events teams.
  4. NegotiateRate card vs. discount, bonus spots, added value.
  5. Book and deliverPO, ad material, schedule, traffic, script approvals.
  6. Prove and reportProof of airing (bukti tayang), digital reports, post-campaign review.
  7. Bill and collectInvoice, faktur pajak, follow up on payment terms. Many media firms pay incentives on collected revenue.
  8. Renew and upsellNext quarter’s plan, event invitations, annual deals.

Role-play

How to pitch a package

If they say “pitch us something”, use these six steps. Start with questions, not products.

  1. Clarify the briefAwareness or leads? Who, where, when, what budget, which KPIs?
  2. Find the insightWhy this audience, why now. Tie it to the news or the economy.
  3. Build the packageTV for reach and credibility, digital for targeting, social for amplification, an event for relationships.
  4. Set the KPIsGRP and reach, views, reads, clicks, leads, attendance.
  5. Price itStart from the rate card, discount within your authority, add value instead of cutting price.
  6. Close with a date“If we confirm by [date], we can secure the slot for your launch month.”

Worked example

Scenario: a national bank launches an SME loan and wants SME owners to know about it within 8 weeks. Insight: SME owners follow economic news because rates rose 100 bps this year, and a trusted news setting makes a bank’s message more believable.

Your turn

Questions to ask them

Ask 3 or 4. The first three work with any interviewer.

  1. Ask first

    How is the sales team organised: by agency, by category, or by direct and government accounts? Which portfolio would this role handle?

  2. Ask first

    Are targets and incentives based on booked revenue or collected revenue, and are they monthly or quarterly?

  3. Ask first

    What does a great first six months look like for someone in this role?

  4. Strategy

    What’s the current mix between TV, digital and events in revenue, and where do you want it to be in two years?

  5. Group

    How much do AEs sell across the group (Trans TV, Trans7, CNBC Indonesia, detik) versus CNN Indonesia only?

  6. Events

    Last year’s CNN Indonesia Awards was on 31 October. Is there an edition coming up this year, and how do AEs get involved in selling events?Check the date isn’t already announced before you ask.

  7. Market

    Which categories are growing fastest for CNN Indonesia this year, and which are you trying to win back?

  8. Editorial

    How does the commercial team work with the newsroom on branded content while protecting editorial independence?

  9. Closing

    Is there anything in my background that makes you hesitate?Gives you a chance to answer doubts while you’re still in the room.

  10. Closing

    What are the next steps, and when can I expect to hear back?
