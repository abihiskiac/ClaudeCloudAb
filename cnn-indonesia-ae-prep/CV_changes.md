## Optional upgrades (only if they're true and you have the numbers)

Specific numbers make sales CVs much stronger. If you know any of these, add them:

- **Target achievement**, e.g. "Achieved 1xx% of 2025 funding target" or "Ranked #x of y CROs in the region".
- **Customer Gathering**: number of attendees and any business that came out of it.
- **Running event**: number of participants, number of editions, sponsors or partners you brought in. Sponsor numbers would be especially good for this role.
- **LaNe Indonesia**: which marketplaces (Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop?), whether you ran paid ads, and results such as ROAS or conversion rate. This shows you've bought ads yourself.
- **Glints**: the size of the ads budget you planned, or a metric that improved.
